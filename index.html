    Question Paper Generator in progress

    A web tool that builds exam papers from a question bank. Shown here with a sample Python Programming (CS201) paper.

    Setting a paper by hand makes it hard to cover every course outcome evenly. This tool takes the paper pattern (sections, question types, counts and marks) and spreads the marks as evenly as possible across all course outcomes before it picks a real question for each slot from a bank of 518 questions. A marks ledger then shows the result per outcome, and the finished paper can be printed or saved.

    • Set up: enter the exam details and upload or paste the syllabus to fill in unit names and outcome text.
    • Balance: marks are split evenly across CO1 to CO5 on every generate.
    • Output: a formatted paper with each question tagged by unit and outcome, and a ledger that confirms the balance.
    Web appQuestion bankOutcome-based educationPython syllabus

    AI Powered Digital Twin for Smart Homes

    B.Tech CSE project (PBL), Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur, 2025-26. Guide: Dr. Sagarkumar S. Badhiye. Team: Manaswi Sakharwade, Samprada Kapate, Sanika Mendjoge.

    A low-cost prototype that mirrors a smart home in a digital twin and automates it with machine learning. Temperature, motion and light sensors feed a microcontroller, and Scikit-Learn models (decision tree, KNN and logistic regression were compared) predict occupancy and room conditions. Relays then switch the lights and the cooling fan, and an LCD on the box shows live temperature, light level and fan speed.

    PythonScikit-LearnESP32 / ArduinoMQTTIoT

    Smart Waste Management System

    B.Tech CSE project (PBL), Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur, 2025-26. Guide: Dr. Sagarkumar S. Badhiye. Team: Manaswi Sakharwade, Samprada Kapate, Sanika Mendjoge.

    An IoT dustbin that tells you how full it is. Ultrasonic sensors measure the fill level, an ESP32 microcontroller reads them, and a servo motor opens and closes the lid automatically. Readings are sent over Wi-Fi to a dashboard, which shows whether the bin is empty, partially filled or full and raises an alert when it needs emptying. The prototype cost about ₹1,200 to ₹1,400 to build.

    • Sense: ultrasonic sensors measure the distance from the lid to the waste.
    • Decide: threshold logic turns the distance into empty, half-filled or full.
    • Alert: the dashboard updates live and flags a full bin so it can be collected on time.
    ESP32 / ArduinoUltrasonic sensorServo motorIoTEmbedded C/C++

    Fire Detection System using Arduino

    Microcontrollers and Embedded Systems course project, Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur.

    A low-cost fire alarm built on an Arduino Uno. A flame sensor module picks up the infrared light given off by a flame, and the Arduino reads it continuously and sounds a buzzer as soon as a flame is detected.

    • Sense: the flame sensor module watches for fire, and its sensitivity is set with the potentiometer on the board.
    • Decide: the Arduino reads the sensor and checks whether a flame is present.
    • Alert: the Arduino switches on the buzzer to raise the alarm.
    Arduino UnoFlame sensorBuzzerEmbedded C/C++

    Automated Home Appliance Control System

    B.Tech CSE project, Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur, 2026-27. Guide: Dr. Parag Naik.

    A home automation system that switches lights, fans and plugs on its own instead of waiting for someone to flip a switch. Motion, light, temperature and gas sensors feed an ESP32 controller, a rule engine decides what each appliance should do, and relays carry out the action. The web dashboard shows every room, lets you override any appliance by hand and lets you create new rules. The dashboard demo runs on simulated sensor data.

    • Observe and analyze: read motion, light level and temperature, and check the current appliance states.
    • Decide and act: rules such as motion plus low light turn a light on, and high temperature turns a fan on, through relays.
    • Update: the dashboard shows the new state and logs every step of the cycle.
